Your services, one app.

Dove sends magnets, .torrent files, hoster links and NZBs to the debrid and Usenet services you already use, then plays the result in Infuse, downloads it, or plays the audiobook itself.

Everything in progress

Bring your own.

Dove ships with no content sources. It works with what you set up yourself:

  • Debrid services: Real-Debrid, TorBox, Offcloud, Debrid-Link, Premiumize, AllDebrid.
  • Usenet: your own nzbdav or InfiniDysk server, or TorBox, Offcloud and Premiumize with Usenet on.
  • Indexers: Newznab and Torznab (Jackett, Prowlarr, Usenet indexers), searched from your device.
  • Stremio addons, such as AIOStreams or Torrentio.
  • Plugins from repositories you add.

Dove has no server of its own. Everything goes straight from your device to the services you configured.

What it does

Find a movie, a season, a book or an album, send it to your services, then watch, listen or download.

Priority with fallback

Put your services in order, separately for torrents and links and for NZBs. “Automatic” tries them in that order and falls through to the next on any error.

Private trackers

Private torrents go only to services that seed. A release one of your services already has cached goes there as a bare magnet, so your ratio does not move.

Search you set up

Movies and TV through TMDB or a Stremio addon, then sources from your addons and indexers, searched in parallel. Books, audiobooks, music and software through your indexers.

Ranked for Apple TV

“Recommended” puts what plays well on Apple TV first, then what is ready, then quality. Filter by resolution, HDR, codec, audio, language and more, or describe what you want in plain words.

Any way in

Paste or share one link or several, a magnet, a .torrent or an .nzb. A movie's page from IMDb, TMDB, Letterboxd, Trakt or JustWatch opens that title with its sources.

Library review

New episodes of shows you follow, gaps in seasons you hold part of, and releases that could be better. Reported to you, never acted on.

Your player

Infuse (with playlists and episode names), VLC, Outplayer, SenPlayer, nPlayer, VidHub, IINA on the Mac, Kodi, or a URL template of your own.

Download to the device

In the background, with pause, resume and a retry with a fresh link. “Download only” adds, waits, downloads the right files and removes the item from the service.

Books where they belong

Send a book to Apple Books, your Kindle or your own Calibre server, or save files to iCloud Drive.

Watchlist

Save movies and series. New episodes, release dates, and a notification when something you saved comes out.

Files and Finder

Your services appear as a location in Files and in Finder: browse, open, delete, or drop a .torrent or .nzb into a service's folder to add it.

Plugins, sandboxed

JavaScript plugins add sources and metadata. They run on your device, reach only the hosts they declare, are pinned by checksum and run only once you enable them. See the repositories.

At home on iPhone

Widgets, Control Center, Live Activity, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight and Quick Actions.

A real Mac app

Native, not Catalyst: sortable source tables, a menu bar extra, Dock progress, drops on the window or the Dock, the Services menu and desktop widgets.

Synced, end to end

Keys, addons, indexers and priorities sync through iCloud Keychain. Export them to a file for installs it does not reach.
